About

Two sides of the same interest.

By day, I'm an AI & Digital Officer at a UK local authority, where I design and build full-stack applications, databases, and automation workflows that support the council's digital transformation, from internal systems to AI training for non-technical colleagues.

By night, I design software for myself and anyone who values keeping their data their own: database-driven applications, encrypted file sharing, and intelligent assistants built on AI models that run entirely on local hardware rather than a third-party cloud.
